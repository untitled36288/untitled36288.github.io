05 — Biography

Jack Yang is a Shanghai-born media artist whose practice engages with Technoetic Aesthetics, cyberception, and bioart, with a particular focus on moistmedia.

His work explores research in biological sciences, chemistry and biochemistry, neuroscience, and their entanglements with Artificial Intelligence. Working across digital media and organic matter, his practice encompasses multiple media and forms.
